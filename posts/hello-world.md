I've been meaning to write for a long time and kept not doing it, mostly because
I couldn't decide what the first post should be. So this is the first post: a
short note about why the blog exists.

## Why write at all

Writing forces a level of clarity that reading never does. It's easy to feel like
you understand a caching strategy or a database index until you try to explain it
in three paragraphs and discover the explanation has a hole in it.

So the plan is simple — when I learn something worth keeping, it goes here.

## What to expect

Mostly short, practical posts:

- things I got wrong and had to debug
- patterns that turned out to be worth reusing
- notes from projects, with the parts that were actually hard

Nothing polished, nothing evergreen. Just working notes that happen to be public.

Thanks for reading.
